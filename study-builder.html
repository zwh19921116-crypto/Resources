What does the study session planner do?

It builds a focused session by matching resources to your stage, topic, available time and study goal.

Can I use this for Primary, Secondary and VCE?

Yes. The planner supports Primary, Secondary and VCE pathways so sessions stay relevant to your level.

How are resources selected?

Matches are filtered from the active library using your selected stage, subject, optional unit and goal type.

What does each goal mean?

Study mixes resource types, Understand prioritises notes, Practise focuses on questions, and Test myself targets exam-style work.
