        Other resources and support

        Helpful materials to guide your learning journey.

        What types of resources are available?

        Edgeducate includes practice questions, practice exams, knowledge sheets, subject checklists, videos, and interactive learning tools across core subjects.

        How should students choose a pathway?

        Start with school stage first, then choose subject and goal. Primary focuses on foundations, Secondary on depth, Selective on readiness, and VCE on performance outcomes.

        Are resources suitable for exam preparation?

        Yes. Pathways are designed to support structured revision, targeted skill practice, and timed exam strategy, especially for senior students.

        Can parents use this page to plan study at home?

        Yes. The pathway summaries are intentionally simple so families can quickly identify the right stream and build consistent weekly study routines.
